// Current Research Final Project · 2026

Mapping urban sprawl in Kabupaten Sukoharjo.

My undergraduate research detects and quantifies indications of urban sprawl across Kabupaten Sukoharjo using the Shannon entropy index, combining multi-temporal satellite imagery and built-up area analysis to measure how compact or dispersed the region's urban growth has become.

  • Shannon entropy index for urban dispersion
  • Multi-temporal built-up area extraction
  • Land cover change detection
  • Spatial pattern & growth direction analysis
